# EndControl Home Hero Design System

## Product context

EndControl Engenharia is a Brazilian industrial engineering company focused on asset integrity, inspections, non-destructive testing and technical consulting. The home hero should communicate technical confidence, safety and operational clarity.

## Brand and colors

- Primary navy: `#00215D` for all primary headings, labels, controls and text on light surfaces.
- Light background: `#F4F9FC` / `#EEF6FA` with white cards and restrained borders.
- Light-blue accent: `#00C2FF` for small accents, icons and active states only.
- Deep image overlay: `#071429` only where needed for readable text over photography.
- Avoid neon gradients, excessive glow, strong blur and decorative shadows.

## Typography

- Inter or Arimo, with the existing site font stack.
- Headlines: bold, compact, navy on light surfaces.
- Supporting copy: muted slate-blue, comfortable line-height.
- Labels: uppercase, letter-spaced, navy.

## Hero composition

- Preserve the existing full-width hero structure, navigation, photographic slide and four-item indicators.
- Favor a light editorial treatment: light content surface with navy text, controlled photo treatment and clear visual hierarchy.
- Use Lucide icons with consistent 1.8–2px stroke, no filters or glow.
- Keep controls and indicators compact, aligned and functional.

## Motion and surfaces

- Use subtle opacity/transform transitions only.
- No animated radar beams, neon scan lines, text glow or large drop-shadows.
- Use small neutral shadows only to separate surfaces from the background.

## Fidelity constraint

Use only the fonts, colors, spacing and component styles defined above and in the existing EndControl CSS. Do not introduce unrelated colors, gradients, decorative visual systems or alternate icon libraries.
